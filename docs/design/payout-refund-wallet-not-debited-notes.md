Notes for `payout-refund-wallet-not-debited` (see design: `payout-refund-wallet-not-debited.design.md`, review: `.claudespace/s/0739740e-5540-4c70-a82e-ba8a55417027/reports/payout-refund-wallet-not-debited-review.md`).

- Built: adopted the complete refund-clawback safeguard from unmerged `feat/refund-card-clawback-safeguard` (`ffc1740`) rather than re-implementing it, then fixed the gross-vs-net debit bug it would otherwise have shipped with, plus a per-appointment idempotency bug in `refund-cancelled-appointment`.
- Non-obvious decision: the wallet debit is derived from the wallet ledger's own credit entries for the transaction, never from the gross refund amount or the tenant's current platform fee — the fee is mutable, so recomputing from it would silently use today's rate against a payment credited under yesterday's.
- Deviation worth remembering: the `complete_transaction_refund` guard needed to call `refund_wallet_debit_amount` itself (not just check for a null debit id) to tell "no credit ever landed, nothing to debit" apart from "a bypass attempt" — both look identical as a null `p_wallet_debit_entry_id` otherwise.
- Confirmed pre-existing/unrelated during review, not fixed here: `CancelSubscriptionDialog.test.tsx`'s third test times out at 5s (last touched by unrelated subscription-lifecycle work); `payments-e2e` cleanup leaks a tenant row when a seeded tenant has a `notification_settings` row (missing `ON DELETE CASCADE`).
- Deferred, left as an explicit seam: `debit_salon_wallet_for_refund` has an unused `p_allow_negative` parameter for the future out-of-band (Paystack-initiated) refund handler (`payments-e2e-refund-webhook-unhandled`), which is the one case where debit-before-effect ordering can't hold.
